# mediating

Generates [mediating-nucleus](content/mediating-nucleus/mediating-nucleus.md) from [`world`](../../world.md): for a family of worlds, a connecting nucleus (M, {Q_i holes}, {ψ_i portals}) — a way-station with degenerate interior regions and well-typed portals into each world. Mediators for a family form a category.
