# thinning

Generates [hole](content/hole/hole.md) from [`world`](../../world.md): a region of a nucleus where the quasicrystalline structure degenerates (H1) and whose outgoing relations all factor through its boundary (H2). A hole is not a world — it is the thinned, lower-complexity substructure through which one world can be reached from another.
