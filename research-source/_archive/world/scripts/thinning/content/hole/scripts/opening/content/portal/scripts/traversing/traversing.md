# traversing

Generates [traveller](content/traveller/traveller.md) from [`portal`](../../portal.md): the fibration of what crosses. For each nucleus the fibre is the category of traveller-states it can support; an ambient morphism (in particular a portal) pushes a traveller-state from one world to another. Travellers are the things-that-can-cross; the fibration lets different worlds carry different traveller types while still admitting transport.
