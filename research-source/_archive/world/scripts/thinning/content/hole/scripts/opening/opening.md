# opening

Generates [portal](content/portal/portal.md) from [`hole`](../../hole.md): a boundary-determined morphism φ : P → W from a hole (pool) into a world. By (PM1) the value at any interior point depends only on its boundary germ — nothing of the hole's deep interior reaches the destination world, only its interface. The portal is the controlled inter-world connection.
