**Sound horizon** $r_d$ is the comoving distance a sound wave travels in the photon–baryon plasma up to the drag epoch — the standard ruler imprinted as baryon acoustic oscillations.
It calibrates the BAO scale, so it is the hinge between early- and late-time pipelines: the CMB fixes $r_d$ from early physics, BAO surveys measure distances *in units of* $r_d$.
Many candidate resolutions of the [hubble-tension](../../../hubble-tension/hubble-tension.md) act precisely by changing $r_d$ (e.g. early dark energy shrinking it) — i.e. by proposing a new [cosmos-cell](../../../../../../../../../classical-spacetime.md) via [cosmogenerating](../../../../../../../../../../../cosmogenerating.md).
