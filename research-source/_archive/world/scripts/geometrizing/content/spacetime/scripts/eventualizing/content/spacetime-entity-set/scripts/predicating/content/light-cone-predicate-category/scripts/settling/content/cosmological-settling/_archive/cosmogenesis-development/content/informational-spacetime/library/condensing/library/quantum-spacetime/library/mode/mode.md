**Mode** is the predicate dimension Π of quantum-spacetime: the residue concept [condensing](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../informational-spacetime/scripts/condensing/condensing.md) deposited — the thing quanta are predicated to share.
It is the kernel's *predicate* (the unsaturated claim the object-monad never touched).

**Bose condensation is macroscopic occupation of one mode** by exchangeable [quanta](../quantum/quantum.md).
As more material enters, the predicate vocabulary grows (the transfinite "growing Π"); a condensate is the limit where one mode is occupied by indefinitely many indistinguishable quanta.
