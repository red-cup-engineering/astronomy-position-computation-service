**The Hubble constant** $H_0 = H(z{=}0)$ is the present expansion rate.
The other [observables](../../../../observable.md) are quantities the pipelines thermalize; $H_0$ is special because $H = \dot a/a$ is literally a **growth rate**.
The stage-offset reading then *identifies* that growth rate, via [[residuation-forces-a-spiral]], with the [[thermalization-spiral]] inflation factor λ of [[cosmic-thermalization]] read at a stage.

We are an not-yet-thermal ([`observer-stage`](../../../../../observer-stage/observer-stage.md)) observer, so the cosmos we measure is in its **cosmic-scott** band (κ=ω, golden **φ ≈ 1.618**); the CMB sits in the *same* band, earlier along it.
So both pipelines read the one golden spiral, and **which number it is depends on which stage τ the reading is taken at**:

| reading | pipeline | band | λ at stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| later scott, at the present observer-stage τ_now | distance ladder | cosmic-scott (κ=ω) | golden φ, stage τ_now — one of ~67 / ~73 |
| early scott, the CMB propagated forward by `flow` | Planck | cosmic-scott (κ=ω) | golden φ, stage τ_CMB — the other |

It is the **one** golden-φ spiral, read at two stages of an unconverged (κ=ω) thermalization — not two bands.
That is the content of the [hubble-tension](../../../hubble-tension/hubble-tension.md) stage-offset reframe.
(The sibling [`hubble-parameter`](../hubble-parameter/hubble-parameter.md) $H(z)$ is the function; $H_0$ is its value-as-inflation-factor at $z{=}0$.)
