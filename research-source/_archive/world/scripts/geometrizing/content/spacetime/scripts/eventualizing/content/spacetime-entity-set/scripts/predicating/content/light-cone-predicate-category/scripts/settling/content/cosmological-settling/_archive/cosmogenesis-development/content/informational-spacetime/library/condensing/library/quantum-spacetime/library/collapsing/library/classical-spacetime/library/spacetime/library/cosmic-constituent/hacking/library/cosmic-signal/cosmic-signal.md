**Cosmic-signal** — how a constituent's charge is carried out to an observer: a coupling that propagates.
A [decouple](../../decouple/decouple.md) freezes a charge into a free signal; thereafter it travels and is what every measurement pipeline reads.
[light](library/light/light.md) (the electromagnetic coupling), the [CMB](library/cmb/cmb.md) (its oldest relic), [gravitational-wave](library/gravitational-wave/gravitational-wave.md) (the □-charge's own ripple in [spacetime](../../../../../spacetime.md)).
Signals carry the redshift (the stage τ) and are the only access measurement has to the field.
