**Hubble parameter** $H(z) = \dot a / a$ is the cosmic expansion rate at redshift $z$; at $z=0$ it is the Hubble constant $H_0$.
It is the subject-coordinate of the cell at the heart of the [hubble-tension](../../../hubble-tension/hubble-tension.md): one fact of the [cosmos](../../../../../../../../../classical-spacetime.md), thermal to ~67 km/s/Mpc by early-time pipelines and ~73 km/s/Mpc by late-time pipelines.
The discordance is the $H^1$ obstruction; the cosmos's actual $H(z)$ is single.
