**ΛCDM** is a **theory** in the sense this branch uses: a *thermal description* — a closure over many [cosmology](../../cosmology.md) cells that projects, from a few parameters ($H_0, \Omega_m, \Omega_\Lambda, \Omega_b, n_s, \dots$), to one consistent assignment of values across the [observables](../observable/observable.md) $H(z)$, $D_A(z)$, $r_d$.

A theory is **not** a [cosmos-cell](../../../../../../../classical-spacetime.md).
The cosmos has the facts; a theory is a recognition-discipline inside [cosmology](../../cosmology.md) that thermalizes many cells coherently. ΛCDM is the discipline both the early- and late-time pipelines assume, which is exactly why their disagreement is sharp: they fit *the same* description to different data and get incompatible $H_0$.

## Role in the tension

ΛCDM is the **null hypothesis** in the predecessor empirical plan: build the $g(z)$ (the $H^1$ value) expected from ΛCDM mock posteriors, then test whether the observed $g(z)$ exceeds it.
A significant excess is evidence the description is incomplete.

## Extensions = candidate cosmos-cells

The natural responses — an evolving equation of state $w(z)$, early dark energy, a modified $r_d$ — are **proposals of new cosmos-cells**: facts ΛCDM did not articulate.
A confirmed extension does not just edit the theory; it enters the cosmos through [cosmogenerating](../../../../../../../../../cosmogenerating.md) as genuinely new world.
The [reflexive-friedmann-system](../reflexive-friedmann-system/reflexive-friedmann-system.md) is one such proposal, built directly from the disequilibrium order parameter.

## Stage-blindness

ΛCDM fits **one converged** growth law to both pipelines — it is stage-blind.
That is exactly *why* it *sees* a [Hubble tension](../hubble-tension/hubble-tension.md): forcing two stage-readings of an *unconverged* (κ=ω, cosmic-scott) spiral — early scott (CMB) and later scott (now) — into a single converged value leaves a residual it must then explain as systematics or new physics.
Under the [stage-offset](../hubble-tension/hubble-tension.md) reading the residual is the expected gap between two stages of one golden spiral that has not yet thermal; ΛCDM's single-converged-value assumption is the source of the puzzle, not a neutral backdrop.
