**Dark-matter** — [matter](../matter/matter.md) (□) whose charge couples to **gravity alone**: pure constraining, no signal coupling.
That purity *is* its character — it clusters (the dominant □-pull, the scaffold [cosmic-structure](../../../../../cosmic-structure/cosmic-structure.md) collapses onto), yet carries to no signal, so it is invisible except through the □ it exerts.
Its "unknown nature" is precisely the absence of any coupling but constraining — a candidate new cell.
