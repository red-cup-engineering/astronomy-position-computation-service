# predicating

Generates [light-cone-predicate-category](content/light-cone-predicate-category/light-cone-predicate-category.md) from [`spacetime-entity-set`](../../spacetime-entity-set.md): the predicate category over E_cos whose morphisms are restricted to those compatible with the causal (light-cone) order — adding light-cone restriction ρ_{e→e'} for e ≼ e' as a fifth generator class. This is relativistic locality made categorical: evidence at e is evidence at e' iff e causally precedes e'.
