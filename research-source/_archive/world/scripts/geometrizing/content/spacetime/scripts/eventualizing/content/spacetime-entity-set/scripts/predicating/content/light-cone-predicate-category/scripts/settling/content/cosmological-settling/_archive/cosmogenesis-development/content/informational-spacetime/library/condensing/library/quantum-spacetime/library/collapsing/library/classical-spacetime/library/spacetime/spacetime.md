**Spacetime** is the **base of the classical presheaf** — the arena the charges live over.
In the descent, classical-spacetime is a valued presheaf; *spacetime* is its base (the cell-set the [constituents](library/cosmic-constituent/cosmic-constituent.md)' charge is assigned over), and its "facts" are the structure on that base: [metric](library/metric/metric.md) (how it measures), [curvature](library/curvature/curvature.md) (how □-charge bends it), [horizon](library/horizon/horizon.md) (how far the relaxation reaches), [vacuum](library/vacuum/vacuum.md) (its ground charge), [observable-universe](library/observable-universe/observable-universe.md) (the part within the horizon).
Spacetime is the carrier; constituents are the charge it carries.
