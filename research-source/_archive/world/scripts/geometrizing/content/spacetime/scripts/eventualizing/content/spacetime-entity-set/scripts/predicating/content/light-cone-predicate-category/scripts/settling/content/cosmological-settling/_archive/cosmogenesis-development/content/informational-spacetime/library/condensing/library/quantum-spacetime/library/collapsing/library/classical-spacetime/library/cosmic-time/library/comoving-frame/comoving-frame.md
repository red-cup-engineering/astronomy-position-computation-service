A **comoving-frame is a section of the [cosmic-time](../../cosmic-time.md) bundle** — the privileged frame in which classical-spacetime's relaxation is read: the foliation into slices of constant cosmic-time, with a worldline through each fixed comoving point.
It is *privileged*, where a generic spacetime has no privileged frame, because it is the frame in which thermalizing looks **symmetric** — invariant under the cosmological principle's spatial isometry.
That symmetry is what lets the one thermalizing have one shared stage-index.

## It grounds the one shared cosmic-time

[cosmic-time](../../cosmic-time.md) asserts that all comoving observers agree on "the universe is 13.8 Gyr old" because there is one thermalizing and so one bound.
The comoving-frame is *why*: comoving observers are the leaves of one foliation, so their clocks read the same stage of the same relaxation.
A **non-comoving** observer reads it *tilted* — the [cmb](../../../spacetime/library/cosmic-constituent/hacking/library/cosmic-signal/library/cmb/cmb.md) acquires a dipole and manifest isotropy is lost.
The dipole-corrected CMB is the working definition of the comoving frame at our location (the Solar System's tilt is ~370 km/s).

## Why the section matters

Without a privileged section, "the same field-cell, one stage later" has no canonical referent, and the stage-to-stage propagation [flow](../../../cosmology/library/flow/flow.md) carries out — the reindexing that takes an early-scott CMB reading forward to the present — would not be well-defined.
The comoving-frame supplies that canonical referent; it is the section that makes flow a functor and fixes what an [observer-instrument](../../../observing/library/observer-instrument/observer-instrument.md) means by "the same place, later."
A change of frame is a morphism between frames; among comoving frames every such change is a spatial isometry, not a boost — the comoving subgroup, not the full Poincaré group.
