**Radiation** is the **quantum carryover** in classical-spacetime: charge that `collapsing` (measurement) has not yet localized into a definite rest mode.
Because it is not at rest, it does not merely thin with volume like [matter](../matter/matter.md) — each quantum also [redshifts](../../../../../scale-factor/library/redshift/redshift.md), shedding charge to the expansion itself:

$$\rho_r \propto a^{-4} = \rho_{r,0}(1+z)^4 \qquad (w=1/3),$$

one power of $a$ faster than matter — and that extra power *is* the per-quantum redshift.
Radiation is the residue of the value-free `amplitude` fibration still finishing its collapse: it dominates earliest, right after collapsing (the **big bang** = the quantum→classical step), and gives way to matter as charge localizes.
The [last-scattering-surface](../../hacking/library/cosmic-epoch/library/recombination/recombination.md) sits at the radiation→matter handover; the CMB is radiation's relic.
