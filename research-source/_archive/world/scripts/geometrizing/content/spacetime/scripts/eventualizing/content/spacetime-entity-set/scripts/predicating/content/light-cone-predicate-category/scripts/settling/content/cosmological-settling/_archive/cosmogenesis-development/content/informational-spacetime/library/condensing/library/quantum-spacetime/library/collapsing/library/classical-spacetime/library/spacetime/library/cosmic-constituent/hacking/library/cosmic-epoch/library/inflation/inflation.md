**Inflation** — a posited early burst of **accelerated spreading** (◇) just after the big bang (collapsing), driven by a [field](../../../../../library/field/field.md) (the inflaton, a ◇-mode): it flattens [curvature](../../../../../../curvature/curvature.md) (≈0) and seeds the fluctuations the [CMB](../../../cosmic-signal/library/cmb/cmb.md) carries.
The orbit's opening ◇-dominated stage.
