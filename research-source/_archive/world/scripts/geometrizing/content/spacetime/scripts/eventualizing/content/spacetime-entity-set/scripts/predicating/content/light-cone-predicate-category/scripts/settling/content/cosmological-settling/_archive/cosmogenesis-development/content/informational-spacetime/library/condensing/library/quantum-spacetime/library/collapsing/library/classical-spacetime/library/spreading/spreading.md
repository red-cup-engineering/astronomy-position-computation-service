**Spreading** is the forward modal generator (◇) on the knowledge order: it raises each cell's charge to the knowledge-**join** across the valuation, appending the raised valuation (the trace stays knowledge-monotone).
It **is** the kernel's `possibilize-knowledge`.

## The derivation

Raising every cell's charge to the join of the accessible evidence is the system reaching toward every state it could occupy — and that is **entropy increase**, by definition of the spread.
Iterated against its right adjoint [constraining](../constraining/constraining.md) (◇ ⊣ □), it is the relaxation toward equilibrium that [thermalizing](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../informational-spacetime/scripts/condensing/content/quantum-spacetime/scripts/collapsing/content/classical-spacetime/scripts/thermalizing/thermalizing.md) takes to its fixed point.
