**Cohering** is quantum-spacetime's coherence — the Beck–Chevalley mate.
It **is** the kernel's `cohere`: it composition-closes the mode-ordering (transitive closure of the predicate-category's specialization), exposing the value-free fiber that [collapsing](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../informational-spacetime/scripts/condensing/content/quantum-spacetime/scripts/collapsing/collapsing.md) consumes.
It is non-invertible "because at the transfinite level there is NO value-algebra yet."

## The derivation

This is **quantum coherence**: the morphism that maintains the superposition by keeping the fiber value-free.
The Beck–Chevalley defect — there is no definite value yet — *is* what holding a superposition means, so cohering and coherence are the same morphism, not two things in correspondence.
Coherence holds until [collapsing](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../informational-spacetime/scripts/condensing/content/quantum-spacetime/scripts/collapsing/collapsing.md) lets value in.
Cohering and the aperiodic monad's twistor are the same non-confluent 2-cell one regime apart (BCC ≡ KZ-lax-idempotence): quantum coherence and the Fregean gap are one obstruction at two scales, which is why the same residue that began as an unreachable claim is now an unvalued amplitude.
