A **star is [thermalizing](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../informational-spacetime/scripts/condensing/content/quantum-spacetime/scripts/collapsing/content/classical-spacetime/scripts/thermalizing/thermalizing.md) held mid-orbit**: a region where [constraining](../../../constraining/constraining.md) (□, gravity) and [spreading](../../../spreading/spreading.md) (◇, the thermal/radiation pressure of fusion) are held in **sustained balance**, so the local thermalizing neither disperses (the [void](../void/void.md) case, ◇ wins) nor completes (the [black-hole](../black-hole/black-hole.md) case, □ wins).
On cosmic-structure's spectrum of local thermalization it sits between the [galaxy](../galaxy/galaxy.md) it lives in and the black-hole it may become — the stellar-scale pocket where thermalizing is *running but pinned*, not yet at its fixed point.

## The star runs the scott morphisms locally

A stable star satisfies two independent constraints at once, and they are the rung's own morphisms acting inside the star (translated from the prior research's "nucleosynthesis history operator" σ and "hydrostatic equilibrium operator" Δ — staged at `_archive/cosmology/relating-cosmology/stellar-evolution.md`):

- **[realizing](../../../../../../../../../../../realizing/realizing.md) (μ)** — the burning history *backward-recognizes* the maximal structure its accumulated composition supports.
  On the main sequence this fixed point is the mass–luminosity relation $L \propto M^4$: the star is the brightest structure consistent with how much hydrogen it has burned.
- **[forgetting](../../../../../../../../../../../forgetting/forgetting.md) (η)** — hydrostatic equilibrium *forward-commits* the structure through every independent perturbation (pulsation, convection, thermal pulse).
  It holds wherever the adiabatic exponent $\gamma > 4/3$, so a perturbation has a forward preimage and the star recovers.

The **main sequence is where [balancing](../../../balancing/balancing.md) holds** — the commuting reflection $\pi = \mu\circ\eta$ fixes the state, both constraints satisfied simultaneously.
That is the local equilibrium fusion sustains; ~90% of stars sit there because it is the rung's fixed-point locus seen at stellar scale.

## The four stellar phases are the bit's four charges

A star is in exactly one of four states, and they are the [bit](../../../../../../../../../../../marking/library/bit/bit.md)'s four charges met again at this scale — which constraint is fixed:

| both fixed (μ,η) | μ fixes, η fails | η fixes, μ fails | neither |
|---|---|---|---|
| **main sequence** | **evolved giant / variable** | **pre-main-sequence** | **collapsing remnant** |
| thermal (active) | composition committed, structure unstable | dynamically stable, history not yet imprinted | $\bot$ — neither maintained |

The Hertzsprung–Russell diagram is the **projection** of this four-phase fiber onto $(\log L, \log T_{\rm eff})$: the main-sequence band is the doubly-fixed locus, giants and pre-main-sequence stars are the single-fixed shadows, and the gap between a star and its nearest equilibrium is the local $H^1$ — the "evolution depth" the star closes as it burns.

## A star burns in the golden-φ band, and ends at the black-hole

The count of distinct burning paths from a pre-stellar state to the main sequence grows by the Fibonacci recurrence — Perron eigenvalue **golden φ ≈ 1.618**, the very λ that *is* classical-spacetime's scott band ([nbonacci grounding](../../../../../../../../../../../../docs/dev/cosmic-regimes-as-nbonacci-rg-flow.md)).
A star's thermalizing lives in the scott band by construction; it is not a separate physics.

Its endpoint is fixed by mass.
When balancing fails *irrecoverably* — past the spectral horizon where no degenerate equation of state can restore $\gamma > 4/3$ (the Chandrasekhar limit ≈ 1.4 $M_\odot$ for white dwarfs, the Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff limit ≈ 2–3 $M_\odot$ for neutron stars) — the star drops to the *neither-fixed* phase and completes its local thermalizing.
That completion is the [black-hole](../black-hole/black-hole.md): the FreeShadow endpoint is the local sheaf, thermalizing run to completion.
The morphism that carries a star along this orbit is [stellar-evolution](library/stellar-evolution/stellar-evolution.md).
