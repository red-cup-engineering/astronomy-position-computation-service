**Neutrinos** — the **transitional** constituent: charge that begins as quantum carryover ([radiation](../radiation/radiation.md)-like, not yet rest-localized) and localizes late, turning matter-like (□) once non-relativistic.
A mode caught mid-collapse — its $w$ slides from $1/3$ toward $0$ as its charge thermalizes to rest.
A small but measured part of the energy budget.
