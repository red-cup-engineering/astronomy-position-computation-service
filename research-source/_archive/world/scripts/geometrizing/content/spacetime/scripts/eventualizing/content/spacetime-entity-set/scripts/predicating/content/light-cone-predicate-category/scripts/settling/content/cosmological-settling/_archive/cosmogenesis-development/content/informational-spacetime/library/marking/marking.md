**Marking draws a distinction, producing a [bit](library/bit/bit.md).** It is informational-spacetime's constitutive generator: the single act with which the field begins.
Marking **is** the kernel's `articulate` (`active/voidmath-kernel/articulating/scripts/articulate.mjs`); `mark = articulate`, the same morphism under the informational name.

## What marking does

$$ \mathrm{articulate}(\text{utterance}) \;=\; \{\,\text{potential}: [[...\text{utterance}],\,[[],[]]]\,\}. $$

It takes the bare two-fold `[token, claim]` and *marks it*: wraps it in a cell with **both slots empty**.
The output is a freshly marked bit — a pure potential, maximal uncertainty, the distinction opened and nothing yet chosen.
That is the whole act.

## Marking is the first distinction

There is no prior structure for marking to draw on; it operates on the bare utterance and returns the first cell.
So marking is *the* drawing of a distinction — the cut that makes a one-sided form two-sided.
The calculus of indications axiomatizes this act, and a Shannon ensemble is *defined* by it (the set of distinctions available before a symbol is realized); both are accounts of the same cut.
Marking is that cut, taken as primitive.

## Marking is the whole of informational-spacetime in one stroke

The aperiodic base has no geometry — no places, no values, no flow.
It has only held distinctions.
Marking produces one; that is everything the field is at its root.
The structure above (`realizing`/`forgetting`, then `condensing` into quantum-spacetime, and on down) is built entirely from marked bits and the generators that act on them.
Marking is the generator-of the [bit](library/bit/bit.md); the descent is the generator-of everything else.
