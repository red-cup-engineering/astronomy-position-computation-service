**Observables** are the base cells of [cosmology](../../cosmology.md): the field-charges a pipeline actually thermalizes, each read through a signal at a stage (redshift).
An observable is the subject-coordinate of a cosmology cell — a charge of classical-spacetime recognized *through* measurement.
A hubble-tension-type obstruction is always a disagreement between two thermalizings of **the same observable**, so observables are the hooks the $H^1$ classes hang on.

> Band-name note: where the members say "cosmic-scott," read **classical-spacetime**; $H_0$ is the thermalization-spiral $\lambda$ read at the present classical stage.

## The canonical base cells

- [hubble-parameter](library/hubble-parameter/hubble-parameter.md) — $H(z)$, the relaxation rate; the cell the tension is about.
- [angular-diameter-distance](library/angular-diameter-distance/angular-diameter-distance.md) — $D_A(z)$, the geometry probe.
- [sound-horizon](library/sound-horizon/sound-horizon.md) — $r_d$, the BAO ruler frozen at recombination's decouple, linking early- and late-time pipelines.
