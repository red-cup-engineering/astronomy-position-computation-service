**Cosmic-time is the thermalizing bound** — the ordinal τ along which classical-spacetime's relaxation runs toward its fixed point.
It is not a background container in which the field evolves; it *is* the stage-index of [thermalizing](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../informational-spacetime/scripts/condensing/content/quantum-spacetime/scripts/collapsing/content/classical-spacetime/scripts/thermalizing/thermalizing.md).
Asking "what time is it" is asking "how many stages of [spreading](../spreading/spreading.md) ∘ [constraining](../constraining/constraining.md) have run."

## Time is thermalizing stepping — not a separate clock

The morphism that advances cosmic-time is thermalizing itself: one application of spreading ∘ constraining, the iterate thermalizing runs to its fixed point.
There is no separate time-generator to mint — that would be a parallel primitive for something the scott rung already does. **Time passing is thermalizing stepping.** The [scale-factor](../scale-factor/scale-factor.md) $a(\tau)$ is a *coordinate of* cosmic-time, not the other way round: $a$ records how far thermalizing has run at stage τ, and its rate $H = \dot a/a$ is the per-stage inflation factor λ — the [friedmann-relation](../spacetime/library/cosmic-constituent/hacking/library/friedmann-relation/friedmann-relation.md) ties that rate to what the field is made of.

## The arrow is the monotonicity of the step

Each thermalizing step lands at least as high in the knowledge order as the last: spreading ∘ constraining is monotone (it climbs, never descends), so a later stage is strictly more determinate than an earlier one.
That one-way determinacy **is** the arrow of time — the past fixed, the future open — and it needs no extra postulate, because thermalizing is directional by construction (its least fixed point is reached by Knaster–Tarski, not by chance).
This is the same arrow thermalizing's gluing realizes *globally* at the fixed point ($H^1 \to 0$); cosmic-time is that arrow seen *per stage*, while the relaxation is still running.

## Why one cosmic-time is shared, and where we are on it

There is one thermalizing, so there is one bound: every comoving reading indexes the *same* relaxation, which is why all comoving observers agree on "the universe is 13.8 Gyr old" — the cosmological principle is the empirical dressing of *one thermalizing, one stage-index*, and the [comoving-frame](library/comoving-frame/comoving-frame.md) is the section that realizes it.
Cosmic-time runs from the [big-bang](../spacetime/library/cosmic-constituent/hacking/library/cosmic-epoch/library/big-bang/big-bang.md) (where [collapsing](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../informational-spacetime/scripts/condensing/content/quantum-spacetime/scripts/collapsing/collapsing.md) first seeded value, τ small) toward the bound, which is reached only when thermalizing completes — [heat death](../thermalizing/library/thermal-spacetime/thermal-spacetime.md), the thermal fixed point.
We read it from inside, mid-relaxation: the present is **scott**, not yet thermal, the bound not yet reached, which is exactly why the field still has an arrow to run and an observer to read it.
Carrying an early-stage reading forward to a later stage is the work of [flow](../cosmology/library/flow/flow.md).
