> **Reframe (merge):** cosmic-scott = **classical-spacetime** (golden φ, not-yet-thermal); cosmic-finite = **thermal-spacetime** (plastic ρ, heat death).
> The anthropic hinge below holds unchanged: an observer exists only in the classical band, never in the thermal (thermal) one.

**Observer-stage** is the stage τ_now of the [[cosmic-thermalization]] that the observer occupies — the observer's own existence treated as a first-class object of [cosmology](../../cosmology.md).

It is the anthropic hinge of the [hubble-tension](../hubble-tension/hubble-tension.md) (regime→Pisot grounding: [`cosmic-regimes-as-nbonacci-rg-flow`](../../../../../../../docs/dev/cosmic-regimes-as-nbonacci-rg-flow.md)). **We are an unconverged thermalization.** Natural existence has not quiesced — the closure orbit is still running, the ω-limit not yet reached — so by definition the cosmos we measure from inside is in its **cosmic-scott** band (κ=ω, golden φ).
We sit *somewhere later along* that convergence, not at its start (the start was the Big Bang, the transfinite→scott generation).

The hinge runs through *us*: an observer can exist **only in the not-yet-thermal regime**.
Full thermalization is the cosmic-finite floor (κ<ω, plastic ρ) — and that floor is **heat death**: nothing left to thermalize, no change, no measurement, no observer.
So the fact that there *is* an observer reading $H_0$ at all forces the reading into cosmic-scott.
We cannot be a finite (thermal) observer; a finite cosmos has no one in it.

Consequence for the tension: the present (late-time) measurement and the CMB (early-time) measurement are **both cosmic-scott readings of the one golden-φ spiral**, taken at different stages τ — early scott vs later scott.
The disagreement is an **intra-scott stage offset**, not a cross-band mismatch, and not a consequence of the observer being "finite."
