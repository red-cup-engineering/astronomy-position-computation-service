**Matter** is [constraining](../../../../../constraining/constraining.md) (□) seen as a constituent.
Constraining lowers charge to the knowledge-meet — the deflationary "what must hold."
A held charge (neither invariant like ◇, nor shed like radiation) is simply spread thinner by the volume the relaxation opens:

$$\rho_m \propto a^{-3} = \rho_{m,0}(1+z)^3 \qquad (w=0).$$

This □-pull **is gravity**: the deflation toward the fixed point.
Matter dominates at intermediate stages — after radiation, before [dark-energy](../dark-energy/dark-energy.md) — and the □→◇ handover is [matter-lambda-equality](../../hacking/library/cosmic-epoch/library/matter-lambda-equality/matter-lambda-equality.md).
It resolves into kinds by *what else* its charge couples to besides gravity: [baryon](../baryon/baryon.md) (couples to signals — measurable), [dark-matter](../dark-matter/dark-matter.md) (pure □, gravity only), [neutrino](../neutrino/neutrino.md) (transitional, radiation-like early).
