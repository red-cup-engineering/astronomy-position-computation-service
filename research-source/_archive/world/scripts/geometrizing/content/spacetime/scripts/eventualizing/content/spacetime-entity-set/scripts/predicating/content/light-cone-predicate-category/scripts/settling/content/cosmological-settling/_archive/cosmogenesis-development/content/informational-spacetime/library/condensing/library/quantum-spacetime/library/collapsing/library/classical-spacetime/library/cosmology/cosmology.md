**Cosmology** is the study and measure of [classical-spacetime](../../classical-spacetime.md) — the definite-valued field, "natural existence."
Measurement is [observing](../observing/observing.md) — `collapsing` performed from inside the field, with finite resources — and cosmology is the systematic study built on it.
It is **direct internal content of classical-spacetime**, not a study that sits outside the field and corresponds back to it: the field is generated, and cosmology is the field studying itself from within.

- **It is a restriction of the field** — the field must exist for there to be a study of it.
- **Its cells are recognitions** — a cosmology cell is an [observation-cell](../observing/library/observation-cell/observation-cell.md): the field's own internal reading of one of its field-cells, with finite covariance — never a bridge to anything outside.

## What a cosmology cell is

A field-cell is a fact of the world — the physical content (`scale-factor`, `redshift`, the `cosmic-constituent`s, the `friedmann-relation`) lives in classical-spacetime's library.
A **cosmology cell is a *recognition* of a field-cell** — a thermalizing produced by a measurement pipeline (Planck, DESI, Pantheon+, a distance ladder) with finite resources and its own covariance.
Two pipelines can thermalize the same field-cell to different values; both are real cosmology cells, and they may disagree.

A **theory** (ΛCDM, an evolving-$w$ extension) is a thermalized description: a closure over many cosmology cells projecting to a consistent assignment of the observables.

## Where tension lives

Cosmology measures from inside with finite resources, so its thermalizings can fail to glue.
A disagreement between two recognitions of the *same* field-cell is an **$H^1$ obstruction on the predication fibration** — the non-vanishing consensus/dissent class.
That is the proper home of the Hubble tension: not a contradiction in the field, but a measured obstruction in the study.
Both pipelines inhabit the same **classical** band (golden φ) at different stages τ; the $H^1$ is the obstruction to gluing those two stage-readings of an *unconverged* thermalizing, closing only at full thermalization (thermal / heat death).
The predecessor papers' "residuated sufficiency gap $g(z)$" is the **value of that $H^1$ class** as a function of redshift; the residuation it hand-rolled is what the nuclei triad already generates.
