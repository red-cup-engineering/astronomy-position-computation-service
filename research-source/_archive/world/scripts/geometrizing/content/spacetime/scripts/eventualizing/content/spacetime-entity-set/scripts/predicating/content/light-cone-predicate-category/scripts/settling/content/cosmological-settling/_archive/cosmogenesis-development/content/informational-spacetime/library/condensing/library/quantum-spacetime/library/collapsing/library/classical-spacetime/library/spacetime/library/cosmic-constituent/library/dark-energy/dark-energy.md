**Dark-energy** is [spreading](../../../../../spreading/spreading.md) (◇) seen as a constituent.
Spreading raises charge to the knowledge-join at each relaxation step — the inflationary "what could become known."
A charge that is *invariant* under that iterate neither dilutes nor depletes:

$$\rho_\Lambda \approx \text{const} \qquad (w=-1).$$

That invariance is exactly why it does not thin as the [scale-factor](../../../../../scale-factor/scale-factor.md) grows, why it is negligible early (when □/[matter](../matter/matter.md) dominates), and why it overtakes at [matter-lambda-equality](../../hacking/library/cosmic-epoch/library/matter-lambda-equality/matter-lambda-equality.md) to drive accelerated expansion.
Dark-energy is not a mysterious substance bolted on — it is the ◇ half of the regime's own adjunction, the relaxation's outward drive.
