A **field** is the carrier beneath a constituent — a `mode` (from quantum-spacetime) before `collapsing` localizes it into a definite charge-bearing constituent.
A constituent *is* a collapsed (definite-charge) excitation of a field.
Modes whose charge is ◇-like drive inflation and are the generic form of [dark-energy](../dark-energy/dark-energy.md) (the inflaton drives [inflation](../../hacking/library/cosmic-epoch/library/inflation/inflation.md)); fields are how constituents are carried on [spacetime](../../../../spacetime.md).
