**Quantum** is the entity dimension E of quantum-spacetime: a quantum, an inhabitant of an [amplitude](../amplitude/amplitude.md).
It is the kernel's *entity* — the saturated object the twistor delivered, carried down by [condensing](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../informational-spacetime/scripts/condensing/condensing.md).

Quanta are **indistinguishable**: many sit in one [mode](../mode/mode.md), and that shared, exchangeable occupation is the Bose condensate.
(Their exchange symmetry is the predicate- category's `converse` involution — the next layer.)
