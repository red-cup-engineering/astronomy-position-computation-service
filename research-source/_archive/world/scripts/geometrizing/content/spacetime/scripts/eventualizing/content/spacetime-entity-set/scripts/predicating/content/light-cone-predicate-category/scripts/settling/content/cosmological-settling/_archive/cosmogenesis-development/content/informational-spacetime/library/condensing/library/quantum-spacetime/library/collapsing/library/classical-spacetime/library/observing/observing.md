**Observing is [collapsing](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../informational-spacetime/scripts/condensing/content/quantum-spacetime/scripts/collapsing/collapsing.md) performed from inside the field, with finite resources, through a signal.** It is not a new primitive and it does not re-derive measurement: it *is* the rung's own value-seeding morphism (posit), restricted by the fact that the observer is itself a cell of classical-spacetime, not a vantage outside it.
Where collapsing seeds value onto the whole fibration, observing reads *one* field-cell's charge through a signal of finite covariance.
It produces an [observation-cell](library/observation-cell/observation-cell.md).

## Measurement is internal — there is no outside to correspond to

The field is *generated*, not mapped onto a pre-existing world: `cosmogenerating ⊢ the real world` ([cosmogenerating](../../../../../../../../../../../../cosmogenerating.md)).
So there is no formal/empirical boundary to bridge and no correspondence to admit.
The observer is a cell of the field, and observing is the field reading its own charge from within — that is the whole content of "measurement" here.
The apparatus below (the cell observing produces, the instrument that fibers it, the links that reconcile instruments) is the descent's own `collapsing` restricted, not a second machine bolted onto an outside.

## Observing is lossy and partial — which is where tension comes from

The observer is inside and finite, so observing cannot land on the full bilattice: it lands on the [observer-instrument](library/observer-instrument/observer-instrument.md)'s **admissible-value sub-bilattice** — what that instrument can witness.
That restriction *is* the selection effect, and it is a property of the instrument, never of the field.
Two observings of the *same* field-cell therefore can disagree, and their disagreement is an $H^1$ obstruction in the study (the [hubble-tension](../cosmology/library/hubble-tension/hubble-tension.md) is the sharpest case), not a contradiction in the field.
Reconciling them is the work of a [calibration-link](library/calibration-link/calibration-link.md).
And prior to any instrument's own cut lies the [cosmic-horizon](library/cosmic-horizon/cosmic-horizon.md) — the ⊥-ceiling no observing can pass, the boundary beyond which the field simply has not reached the observer.
