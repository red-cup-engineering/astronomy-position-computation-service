**Baryons** — ordinary matter (protons, neutrons): [matter](../matter/matter.md) (□) whose charge *also couples to signals*.
Beyond the gravitational □-pull, baryon charge carries to [light](../../hacking/library/cosmic-signal/library/light/light.md) and the [CMB](../../hacking/library/cosmic-signal/library/cmb/cmb.md) — which is exactly why it is the **measurable** fraction: stars, gas, the luminous world cosmology actually reads. $\Omega_b$ is fixed by [nucleosynthesis](../../hacking/library/cosmic-epoch/library/nucleosynthesis/nucleosynthesis.md) and the CMB.
