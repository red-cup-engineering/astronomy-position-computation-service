**Balancing** is the scott coherence — and unlike the earlier coherences (cohering, the twistor) it is **invertible**: ◇□ = □◇.
It **is** the kernel's `commutate`: it splits the field into `{saturated: presheaf, residue: traces}` — the value-saturated substrate vs. the unglued gluing that [thermalizing](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../informational-spacetime/scripts/condensing/content/quantum-spacetime/scripts/collapsing/content/classical-spacetime/scripts/thermalizing/thermalizing.md) consumes.

## The derivation

The confluence ◇□ = □◇ is **detailed balance**: the reversible, time-symmetric local laws commute, so a single global equilibrium exists to glue to, and that is exactly *why the equilibrium thermalizing reaches is unique*.
This invertibility — absent at the twistor and at cohering — is what lets the residue finally drain to zero ($H^1 \to 0$).
Balancing is therefore the step that makes the cascade *terminate*: only a confluent comparison can close the obstruction the prior three regimes could only pass downward.
