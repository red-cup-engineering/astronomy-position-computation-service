**Decouple** — a [constituent](../../cosmic-constituent.md)'s charge ceasing to couple to a signal as the field cools: photons at [recombination](../library/cosmic-epoch/library/recombination/recombination.md), neutrinos earlier.
The act that **releases a [signal](../library/cosmic-signal/cosmic-signal.md)** (the CMB) — a coupling switching off, freezing into the signal whatever the charge last held.
