**Cosmic-constituent** is the charge-bearing content of classical-spacetime.
A constituent is a `mode` (from quantum-spacetime) that [collapsing](measurement) gave a **definite charge**, now carried in the valued presheaf.
What distinguishes the constituents is *how their charge behaves under the relaxation* — the iterate [spreading](../../../spreading/spreading.md) (◇) ∘ [constraining](../../../constraining/constraining.md) (□) that [thermalizing](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../informational-spacetime/scripts/condensing/content/quantum-spacetime/scripts/collapsing/content/classical-spacetime/scripts/thermalizing/thermalizing.md) drives to the sheaf.
That behavior is exactly the equation-of-state $w$, with $\rho \propto a^{-3(1+w)}$ over the relaxation stage $a$ = [scale-factor](../../../scale-factor/scale-factor.md):

- [dark-energy](library/dark-energy/dark-energy.md) — the **spreading** term (◇), $w=-1$: charge invariant under the iterate; it does not dilute; it *is* the inflationary drive.
- [matter](library/matter/matter.md) — the **constraining** term (□), $w=0$: charge held, diluted only by the volume the spread opens.
  This is gravity — "what must hold."
- [radiation](library/radiation/radiation.md) — the **quantum carryover**, $w=1/3$: charge not yet rest-localized; it both spreads *and* redshifts (sheds charge to the expansion), so it dilutes fastest, and dominates earliest — just after collapsing (the big bang).

## The cosmic balance *is* the modal adjunction

matter (□) deflates toward the fixed point; dark-energy (◇) inflates away from it.
Their tension is not two substances in competition — it is the scott regime's **◇ ⊣ □ adjunction**, read cosmologically.
The matter→Λ crossover [matter-lambda-equality](hacking/library/cosmic-epoch/library/matter-lambda-equality/matter-lambda-equality.md) is where the iterate crosses from □-dominated to ◇-dominated: the sign change in the relaxation, the critical point of the cosmic RG flow.
The [friedmann-relation](hacking/library/friedmann-relation/friedmann-relation.md) is the bookkeeping of that balance — how the charges sum into the relaxation rate $H$.

(Matter resolves into kinds — `baryon`, `dark-matter`, `neutrino` — by *what else* their charge couples to besides gravity; `field` is the carrier a constituent is a definite excitation of.)
